Group Task

Build a Provisional Narrative

Using the completed Evidence Chart, groups develop a short response to the investigation question using at least three sources that represent different perspectives.

1. ClaimState what the source set suggests about American experiences and responses.
2. EvidenceSelect evidence from at least three sources.
3. ConnectionExplain how the evidence works together.
4. ComplicationIdentify evidence that limits, challenges, or complicates the claim.
Teacher Does

Ask groups to sort evidence into the themes above, then circulate as students construct their interpretation. Press students to identify the specific source supporting each statement and challenge broad claims that treat all Americans as responding in the same way.

Students Do

Review the Evidence Chart, group related evidence, and create a provisional narrative using at least three sources from different perspectives. Students must explain both what the sources reveal and how one source complicates or limits another.

Check for Understanding

Each group presents its provisional claim. Another group responds with one question or source that could strengthen, challenge, or complicate that interpretation. Students revise the claim if the evidence requires it.

Investigation Question

What do different sources reveal about how Americans experienced and responded to the Vietnam War?
