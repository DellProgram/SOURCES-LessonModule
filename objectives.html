Student Learning

Objectives

01 DOK 3 · Strategic Thinking

Analyze primary and secondary sources about the Vietnam War by identifying the creator’s perspective, purpose, and message and supporting their interpretation with specific evidence.

02 DOK 3 · Strategic Thinking

Compare and evaluate different American perspectives on the Vietnam War, identifying agreements, disagreements, and limitations among the sources.

03 DOK 3 · Strategic Thinking

Draw conclusions about how events and developments during the Vietnam War influenced the ways Americans experienced and responded to the conflict, using evidence from multiple sources.

04 DOK 4 · Extended Thinking

Synthesize evidence from at least three primary and secondary sources to construct and defend an evidence-based response to the investigation question.

What do different sources reveal about how Americans experienced and responded to the Vietnam War?
