Evaluate the Lesson

Determine Instructional Effectiveness

Review students' Evidence Charts, discussion responses, and final writing for patterns. The lesson is effective when students can distinguish perspectives, corroborate evidence, and support a conclusion with multiple sources rather than treating one source as the complete story.

Common misunderstandings should guide reteaching, additional modeling, or changes to the source sequence before the lesson is taught again.

Alignment

The final response directly measures the lesson's DOK 4 objective by requiring students to synthesize evidence from multiple primary and secondary sources into an evidence-based historical interpretation.
