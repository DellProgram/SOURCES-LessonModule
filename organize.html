Teacher Does

Introduce the Vietnam War Evidence Chart and model how the opening photograph fits into the organizer. Emphasize that students should record evidence separately from interpretation.

Students Do

Add the rally photograph to the chart, then write a short initial hypothesis about how Americans responded to the Vietnam War. Students also identify at least one question the photograph cannot answer.

Check for Understanding

Ask students to share one evidence-based idea and one limitation or unanswered question. Listen for students separating what the source shows from what they are inferring.

Initial Hypothesis

Based on the evidence so far, what might this source suggest about American responses to the Vietnam War?

Source Limitation

What can this photograph not tell us about Americans' experiences or responses?
