Day 1 · About 8 Minutes

Scrutinize

Teacher Does

Display the rally photograph without first explaining its title, date, or historical context. Ask students to study the image silently before discussion.

Students Do

Record what they know from direct observation, what they think the source suggests, and what they wonder after examining the image.

Check for Understanding

Ask students to identify one statement that is an observation and one that is an inference. Collect several unanswered questions to revisit later in the investigation.

Know What can you directly observe?
Think What do you think the source suggests?
Wonder What questions does the source raise?

After the initial observation: reveal the source information and introduce the investigation question: What do different sources reveal about how Americans experienced and responded to the Vietnam War?
