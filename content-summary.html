Content Summary

What the Teacher Should Know

Students will encounter sources that do not present one unified American response to the war. The lesson moves from public protest and government policy to political criticism, My Lai, and the secret bombing of Cambodia. The goal is for students to compare perspectives, evaluate evidence, and explain how disagreement and disillusionment developed across the source set.

Before teaching, review the background materials so the source excerpts can be placed in their historical and political context without giving students a predetermined conclusion.
