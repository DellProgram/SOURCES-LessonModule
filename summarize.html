Individual Response

Answer the Investigation Question

What do different sources reveal about how Americans experienced and responded to the Vietnam War?

01

State a clear, defensible claim.

02

Use evidence from at least three sources.

03

Include at least two different perspectives.

04

Explain how the evidence supports, complicates, or limits the claim.

Teacher Does

Direct students back to their opening notes and Evidence Chart. Remind them that the goal is to make an evidence-based interpretation rather than list every source. Confer briefly with students who need help connecting evidence to a claim.

Students Do

Compare their initial thinking with what they learned across the investigation, then write an individual response using the required evidence and perspectives. Students should explain how their understanding developed across the source set.

Check for Understanding

Before submission, students underline their claim, identify the three sources they used, and mark where they explained a difference, complication, or limitation in the evidence. The teacher uses these checks to identify any remaining gaps in sourcing or synthesis.

Final Reflection

What is one question about the Vietnam War or American responses to it that you would still want another source to help answer?
