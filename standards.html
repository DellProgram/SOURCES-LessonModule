Primary Content Standard

SS.912.6.14

Analyze causes, course, and consequences of the Vietnam War.

Students examine political decisions, military events, public protest, controversy, and changing American responses to the war.

Secondary Inquiry Standard

SS.912.A.1.4

Analyze how images, symbols, objects, cartoons, graphs, charts, maps, and artwork may be used to interpret the significance of time periods and events from the past.

Students interpret photographs and antiwar visual sources as historical evidence and compare them with written primary and secondary sources.
